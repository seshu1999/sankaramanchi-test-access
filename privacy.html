Sankaramanchi Astrology – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Sankaramanchi Astrology app ("the app", "we") collects, why, and how you can have it deleted. The app is run by the Sankaramanchi family. Questions: sseshadrisarma@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and mobile number, used to sign you in with a one-time code sent by SMS or WhatsApp.
  • Birth and family details you enter: date, time and place of birth, gender, gotra, caste, marital status, spouse's name and the same details for family members you add. These are used to prepare your horoscope, Panchang, matching, sankalpam and other readings.
  • Location: your approximate location (city), only if you allow it, to give correct Panchang timings for where you are. Precise location is used only inside the Vastu tool while you walk the corners of a plot, and only if you allow it.
  • Camera and microphone: used only when you choose to take a photo, use the Vastu camera view, or send a voice message to Appaji. Nothing is recorded in the background.
  • Photos and files you upload: such as a horoscope or document you send for a reading.
  • Messages with Appaji: your questions and voice messages in the Appaji chat, so they can be answered.
  • Notification token: a device token so we can send you the reminders and readings you asked for.
  • Feedback you choose to send us.

We do not collect contacts, call logs, SMS messages, payment details or advertising IDs, and the app shows no ads.

How we use it

Only to run the app's features for you: preparing readings and timings, answering your questions, sending the notifications you chose, and keeping your account secure. We do not sell your information or use it for advertising.

Who else handles it

We do not share your information with anyone. A few trusted service providers work for us only to run the app, such as delivering notifications to your phone and sending sign-in codes by SMS or WhatsApp. They may use your information only for that and not for their own purposes. We disclose information only if the law requires it.

Storage and security

Your information is stored on our own server and sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Sign-in tokens on your phone are kept in the phone's secure storage.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your information while you have an account. You can delete your account at any time:

  • In the app: open Profile and tap Delete account. Your account is deleted within 7 days (sooner if our office approves it first). You can change your mind and keep the account until then.
  • Without the app: email sseshadrisarma@gmail.com with the mobile number you registered. We delete it within 7 days and reply when it is done.

Deleting removes your app account, your birth and family details and the family members you added, your Appaji chats and voice messages, files you uploaded, notifications and sign-in sessions. If you have also visited our office, the records of those consultations (for example a printed Jatakam) are kept as office records. Uninstalling the app removes what is stored on your phone.

Children

The app is not meant for children under 13. Parents may enter a child's birth details as a family member to prepare readings for them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
